YO. POP QUIZ.

You're walking home from school. A car pulls up. The window rolls down. A gun comes out, and so does a multiple-choice math question, at your grade: kindergarten to SAT prep.

Get it right and you keep walking. Get it wrong and, well: YOU LOSE, shoulda listened to your math teacher.

FEATURES

  • Pick your grade: kindergarten, 1st to 8th grade, high school or SAT prep. Every grade covers its Common Core standards and the rest of what that grade's classes do: counting and shapes, place value, clocks and coins, times tables, fractions, decimals, ratios, equations, functions, geometry, statistics.
  • Endless original questions: fresh numbers every time, pixel-art clocks, coins, base-ten blocks, number lines, triangles, graphs and charts, and wrong answers that are the ones you were about to pick.
  • Read aloud for kindergarten to 2nd grade, so the littlest players can play too.
  • The chalkboard of shame. Every time you die, Mr. Delgado works the problem out for you. He's not mad. He's disappointed.
  • Power-ups found in trash cans, like all the best school supplies: the TI-83 crosses out two wrong answers, coffee runs the quiz clock at half speed, and the Kevlar Trapper Keeper stops one bullet. (You still got it wrong, though.)
  • Teacher's Pet streaks for double, triple and quadruple points.
  • Fully voiced, in glorious 8-bit: a hype-man announcer, a gunman with strong opinions about your GPA, and a hero who keeps leaving things at school.
  • A report card at game over (or your estimated SAT Math score), and a top-ten table with three-letter initials, as nature intended.
  • Parent Mode: press P on the title screen and every gun becomes a water pistol. The math stays real.

CONTROLS

  • 5 insert coin, 1 or Enter start
  • ← → walk, Z kick trash cans
  • A B C D or 1–4 answer (or the arrows and Z)
  • X use a TI-83, R read the question again (K to 2nd)
  • P Parent Mode, M mute, F full screen, Esc pause
  • On a phone or tablet, an arcade panel appears: arrows to walk, A and B, plus COIN, START, SOUND, PAUSE and READ. Tap an answer to pick it. Play sideways for the biggest screen.
